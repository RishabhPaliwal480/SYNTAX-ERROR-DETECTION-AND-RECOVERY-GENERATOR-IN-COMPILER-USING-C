                        FORMAL GRAMMAR SPECIFICATION
                    

                    

                        LL(1) Productions, FIRST/FOLLOW & Recovery Matrix.
                    

                

                

                    Directly implements the future-enhancement specification from the RTU Kota synopsis report: explicit FIRST/FOLLOW sets and predictive parsing decisions.
                

            


            
            

                
                

                    

                        1. Left-Recursion Eliminated Expression Grammar
                    

                    

                        

                            Stmt → E ;
                            parseStatement()
                        

                        

                            E → T E'
                            parseExpression()
                        

                        

                            E' → + T E' | - T E' | ε
                            parseExpressionPrime()
                        

                        

                            T → F T'
                            parseTerm()
                        

                        

                            T' → * F T' | / F T' | ε
                            parseTermPrime()
                        

                        

                            F → ( E ) | id | num
                            parseFactor()
